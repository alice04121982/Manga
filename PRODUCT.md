# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Proposed in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md and still under review: Next.js website on Vercel, Expo (React Native) apps for iOS and Android, Supabase backend. The native apps are planned, not yet scoped for design language per OS. Layouts are drawn as web at phone and desktop widths with one shared identity.

## Users
Primary: buyers and collectors who know the manga they want and need to find the right edition (single volume, 2-in-1, 3-in-1 omnibus, box set) and the best place to buy it. Secondary: sellers clearing a shelf who do not know what a set is worth. Launch market is likely the UK in pounds, not yet confirmed.

## Product Purpose
One central place to find any manga, see what each edition looks like and what format it is, and compare prices across the shops and marketplaces that already sell it, plus copies listed by people on the platform. Sellers can list a copy with photos and get a suggested price. Success is a buyer reaching the cheapest trustworthy offer in a few taps.

## Positioning
A manga-specific catalogue and price comparison that understands editions and formats (omnibus versus single volumes), which general price comparison sites do not. A light check found no manga-specific marketplace with price comparison; this is not proper market research.

## Operating Context
Used on phones while shopping or browsing, and on the web via search. Shops and marketplaces appear through feeds and affiliate links, user listings through photos and barcode scans. Buyers complete purchase on the other site for aggregated offers.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Catalogue split into series, edition and volume. Offers ranked by total cost including postage, with a best offer tag. User listings with condition, photos and price. Suggested price for sellers. Search and filters, saved items and price alerts. Payments and messaging are later phases. Not decided: app name (placeholder in designs), launch country and currency, whether serialised magazine issues are in scope.

## Brand Commitments
The user asked for the look to be in the style of manga. No name, logo or palette is chosen. A placeholder name is used in mockups.

## Evidence on Hand
No real catalogue, prices, covers, shop feeds or users yet. Designs must use clearly invented sample titles and prices, and must not reproduce real manga covers or characters.

## Product Principles
1. Price and format clarity come before decoration.
2. Aggregated shop offers and user listings are always labelled so buyers know who sells what.
3. Uncertain matches are shown honestly, never presented as certain.
4. Be generous with manga character in headings, covers and empty states, and calm on prices and forms.
5. Safe to use: no personal details exposed, clear reporting for suspect listings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific standard stated. Assume WCAG AA contrast and readable price text, given the strong graphic style.
